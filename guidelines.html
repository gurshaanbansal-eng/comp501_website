Group 32 Working Agreement & Contribution Standards

Group Guidelines

These guidelines outline how Group 32 operates, communicates, and contributes to ensure a fair, productive, and respectful working environment throughout the COMP501 project.

Communication

  • All group members must respond to messages within 24 hours.
  • Primary communication platform: WhatsApp
  • Major decisions must be agreed upon by all members before implementation.
  • Be respectful, constructive, and professional in all group interactions.

Meetings

  • Meetings are held Weekly before 2 - 4pm Class on Campus.
  • All members are expected to attend unless prior notice is given.
  • Meeting minutes must be recorded and added to the website after each session.
  • Agenda items should be shared before each meeting where possible.

Contributions & Responsibilities

  • Each member is responsible for their assigned section (see Team Members page).
  • Work must be submitted to the group at least [ Insert days ] before the deadline.
  • All content must be original or properly cited in APA7 format.
  • Members should peer-review each other's sections before final submission.

Website & Code Standards

  • All HTML files must use the shared mystyles.css stylesheet.
  • Pages must be tested in a browser before being considered complete.
  • File and folder names must use lowercase with no spaces.
  • All images must include descriptive alt attributes for accessibility.

Conflict Resolution

  • Disagreements should first be raised within the group and resolved through discussion.
  • If unresolved, the issue may be escalated to the course lecturer or tutor.
  • All members agree to act in good faith and prioritise the project's success.
