Social Media Algorithms

Opportunities, Risks and Choices for Society — COMP501 Group 32 · AUT 2026

Every time you open Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube, an algorithm decides what you see. These systems track what you watch, skip, like, and share — then use that data to keep you scrolling. This project explores the opportunities they create, the risks they pose, and the choices we have as a society.
